Smartsheet Dashboard Overlay

Choose which grid layouts are drawn on the dashboard editor and manage your own custom layouts. You can also switch layouts on and off from the extension's toolbar popup while viewing a dashboard.

Custom Layout

Describe the layout as a comma-separated list of JSON objects, each giving a rectangle's color, opacity, height, width, x and y in dashboard grid squares. For example: 

{"color": "Red", "opacity": "20%", "height": 10, "width": 6, "x": 3, "y": 7}

This draws a red rectangle at 20% opacity covering 10 squares down by 6 across, with its top-left corner on the 4th square from the left and the 8th square from the top.

  • The top-left grid square is x = 0, y = 0, not 1, 1.
  • A height of -1 extends the shape downwards indefinitely.
  • A width of -1 extends the shape to the right indefinitely.

Full example

{"color": "Red", "opacity": "20%", "height": -1, "width": 6, "x": 3, "y": 0},
{"color": "Green", "opacity": "30%", "height": 30, "width": 10, "x": 10, "y": 15},
{"color": "Blue", "opacity": "50%", "height": 20, "width": -1, "x": 0, "y": 10}
